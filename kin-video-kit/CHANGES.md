# Changes from the kit as supplied

The four documents contradicted each other in places where following one rule broke another. This version makes them agree. The items marked **choice** are calls I made where the documents did not decide; change them if they are wrong.

1. **Script length.** 170 to 230 words at 3.3 words a second runs 52 to 70 seconds, past the 60 second cap. Now 150 to 200 words, which is 45 to 60 seconds at the house rate.
2. **Captions vs "never before".** The faint caption line is now the only text allowed on screen before its word is spoken. Full-strength text never leads the word, and the line's fade starts on the spoken frame.
3. **Delivery tracks.** The delivery prompt asked for 6 tracks and dropped the cut-out, captions and music. It now lists the same 9 as the standard, with the video order written out (bottom to top: face, footage, text behind, cut-out, text in front, captions).
4. **Resolve timeline per version.** The standard and the notes prompt said every version is a Resolve timeline, but the pipeline only built one after the review loop. The pipeline now builds it at every version (stage 14).
5. **One stage numbering.** PROMPTS and PIPELINE now share one 15-stage list. Reference, story, script and beat map were missing from the pipeline.
6. **Beat map before the lock** (choice). The beat map now runs on the editor's offline cut, so pacing fixes that need an A-roll change happen before swaps are paid for.
7. **Swap only what is seen** (choice). Only the A-roll shots the beat map keeps on screen are swapped. Shots fully under a card or full-frame footage stay green.
8. **The lock.** Notes after the lock may change text, inserts, captions, sound and grade. A note that moves an A-roll cut, or removes an insert over unswapped A-roll, is marked not done with the shots that would need swapping again.
9. **Sound priority** (choice). When two effects would land within 0.16 s, the stronger wins: special sound, then headline hit, then cut whoosh, then small-line tick.
10. **Centre** (choice). The safe area centres at x 497.5, y 840, not the frame centre. Cards, lists and diagrams centre on the safe area. Text tied to the presenter centres on the presenter, then moves in to fit.
11. **True peak** is written as dBTP.

Not in this repo: `Editing/`, `After Effects/`, `DaVinci Resolve/`, `Example data/` and the two Claude skills. They were not part of what was supplied, so nothing in them has been checked against these changes.
